About ToxicDownloader

A simple social media downloader

ToxicDownloader is a web-based media utility built around a simple idea: paste a supported public URL, preview the media information, choose a quality and download the file.

The interface is intentionally lightweight and responsive so the same workflow feels comfortable on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Availability depends on the public source, platform restrictions and the media formats returned by the platform. ToxicDownloader does not guarantee that every URL or every resolution will always be available.
