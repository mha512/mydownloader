Legal and Privacy

Privacy Policy

Learn how ToxicDownloader processes public media URLs, temporary files, technical information and advertising related data.

ToxicDownloader provides a web-based service for processing publicly accessible media URLs. The service is designed to work without requiring users to submit account passwords, authentication cookies, payment details or other sensitive credentials.

When you submit a URL, the application may process the address and request publicly available media information from the supported platform or media service. Temporary files may be created on the server when needed to prepare a requested download.

ToxicDownloader may collect standard technical information such as request time, error information, browser-related information and server logs for security, reliability, abuse prevention and service maintenance. The exact retention period depends on the server configuration.

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable choices or controls provided by the advertising platform.

Do not submit private credentials or information that you do not want processed. Use the service only for content that you are authorized to access and download.
