How to Download Public Videos

What should you check before submitting a link, and why can a public-looking URL still fail?

1. Confirm the video is public

Open the URL in a private browser window without signing in. If the source asks for an account, restricts the audience, or reports that the post is unavailable, ToxicDownloader cannot bypass that access control.

2. Use the platform URL itself

Submit a direct public URL from a supported platform. Short links and redirects work only where supported by the current extractor. Do not submit passwords, cookies, or private credentials.

3. Review the returned preview

ToxicDownloader first extracts metadata and available formats. The quality choices are not promises of every resolution; they reflect what the current source returned for that specific video.

4. Download only content you may use

You are responsible for permission, copyright, and the platform's terms. Public visibility does not automatically grant permission to republish or redistribute a video.
